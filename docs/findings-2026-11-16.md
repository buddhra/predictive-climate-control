# Findings, 2026-11-16: more closed stretches, and the open scenarios at night

Follows `findings-2026-11.md`. **Nothing here is deployed.** Reproduce with `python3 backtest/evaluate.py` and `python3 backtest/evaluate_open.py`.

## Closed scenario: the hypothesis holds on new data

Six clean closed stretches were added (`backtest/more_stretches.py`), for 9 stretches on 8 days and 49 hourly points: one summer day (Jul 16), one overcast fall day (Oct 20), four clear fall days, and one fall evening.

| Coefficients | Squared error, all | Fall | Jul 16 | Worst hour | Jul 16 at 5pm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current | 109.0 | 61.7 | 47.3 | 3.8 °F | +3.8 °F |
| Findings-doc candidate | 21.3 | 6.9 | 14.4 | 2.5 °F | +2.5 °F |
| Candidate with UV gain 0.10 | 11.0 | 6.2 | 4.8 | 1.1 °F | +0.9 °F |
| Refit on all nine | 9.3 | 5.2 | 4.1 | 1.1 °F | +0.2 °F |

- The candidate was fitted to three stretches. On the five fall stretches it had not seen, every end-of-stretch miss is within 0.7 °F.
- The current formula runs 3.8 °F warm by 5pm on Jul 16. The UV term adds about 1.3 °F per hour at a UV index of 10. Lowering the UV gain fixes that day and does not change fall, where the solar floor is larger than the UV term.
- Refit: outdoor rate 0.015, no mass-to-air term, solar floor 0.5 centred on 3pm, UV gain 0.08.
- Leaving each stretch out and refitting gives the same values six times out of nine. The three that change are Nov 7, Nov 15 and Jul 16.

Limits: summer is one day; behaviour after 6pm rests on two days (Nov 7, Nov 15); there is no cold cloudy closed day and no closed night, because the furnace runs on those.

## Open scenarios: the current formula cools far too fast at night

Six clean open nights were pulled (`backtest/open_stretches.py`): two with only the skylights open, four with the skylights and the bedroom window open. Fan at 0 W and furnace idle on all six.

| Night | Really open | Hours | Bedroom change | Current formula | Miss at end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 1 | skylights | 11 | −0.9 °F | −9.4 °F | −8.5 °F |
| Nov 8 | skylights | 11 | −1.0 °F | −7.5 °F | −6.5 °F |
| Nov 7 | skylights + window | 9 | −6.1 °F | −6.7 °F | −0.6 °F |
| Nov 9 | skylights + window | 10 | −5.7 °F | −7.7 °F | −2.0 °F |
| Nov 10 | skylights + window | 9 | −5.8 °F | −8.5 °F | −2.8 °F |
| Nov 13 | skylights + window | 11 | −2.8 °F | −8.7 °F | −5.9 °F |

- **Skylights alone barely cool the bedroom overnight.** With outside 45–57 °F the bedroom lost about 0.1 °F per hour on both nights. The formula expects about 0.8 °F per hour. Two nights.
- **The bedroom window is what cools it:** 0.6–0.7 °F per hour on three nights. On the fourth (Nov 13) it lost 0.25 °F per hour with the sensor reading open; the window may have been only cracked. The sensor is open/closed, so the formula cannot tell.
- **The base rate is the problem, not the venting amounts.** With the current 0.05 base, the best fit pushes both venting amounts negative. With the closed refit's 0.015 base the fitted amounts are small: skylight −0.012 (so about 0.003 in total), window 0.013.
- **Raising the venting amounts to cancel the lower base makes things worse** (squared error 991 vs 868 for the current formula). The current overnight totals should not be preserved.
- Even the closed refit loses heat too fast overnight: scored as closed, the two skylight nights end 1.4 and 2.4 °F low. The bedroom holds heat at night better than a single outdoor rate allows. That points at a real mass state (or occupants' heat) and is a formula change, not a coefficient.

Squared error on the six nights: current 868, closed refit with venting unchanged 155, closed refit with venting fitted to these nights 66.

Limits: six nights, all in the first two weeks of November with outside 45–60 °F; no fan night; no open stretch in the afternoon or early evening, which is when the skylight automation actually decides; one window sensor, not an opening size.

## Where this leaves the fix

- The closed-scenario coefficients are supported by 8 days and could be proposed as option values.
- The open scenarios cannot be compensated by arithmetic. They need their own values, and the skylight scenario needs afternoon and evening stretches before any value is trusted.
- Still to do: afternoon and evening open stretches, a fan night, more summer days, and a decision on a mass state for overnight.
